About

A demo of the Apple Hardware Bridge Component for Hotwire Native.

What the pages show

  • The device: its model, its name and whether it is a phone or an iPad
  • The safe area, and what covers the screen, such as the Dynamic Island
  • On the iPhone Duo: the two screens, the hinge and the fold

Around the pages the app uses standard iOS navigation: a navigation bar, tabs and a + button.
